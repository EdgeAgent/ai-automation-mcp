# Triage labels

- **urgent** — time-sensitive and consequential: a client blocked on
  something, a deadline inside 24 hours, money or access at risk, a
  human explicitly waiting on the user.
- **routine** — a real message that deserves a reply but keeps: normal
  questions, scheduling, updates, introductions, FYIs with a question
  attached.
- **spam** — unsolicited bulk, scams, SEO/link pitches, phishing,
  automated noise, and anything whose only goal is to get the user to
  click, pay, or reply against their interest.

Tie-breakers: urgent beats routine; routine beats spam. A message from
a known client is never spam, however short it is. A message that
impersonates a known sender is spam *and* gets flagged in the handoff
as a possible phishing attempt.
